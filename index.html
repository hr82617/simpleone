About Us

Electric scooters are plug-in electric vehicles with two wheels. The electricity is stored on board in a rechargeable battery, which drives one or more electric motors. Electric scooters have a step-through frame.

The electric scooter will come in two variants. Both variants of the Electric scooter feature a similar design that comprises a compact twin-pod headlight with an LED DRL ring, apron-mounted sleek LED indicators, a body-coloured front fender, curvy side panels, a sleek LED taillight, an external charging point at the back, and a split-style pillion grabrail.

The Electric scooter features a 36-litre under-seat storage that can accommodate two open-face helmets. The color palette for the base model includes five options while the Pro variant is available in 10 choices.
